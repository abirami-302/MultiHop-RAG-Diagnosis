Empirical Diagnostic Study

Diagnosing the Multi-Hop Retrieval-Generation Gap

A Stage-Wise Error Budget on HotpotQA (N=500, 19,260-Passage Distractor Pool)

Paper Core Finding: On HotpotQA (N=500), a cross-encoder reranker yields the single largest retrieval gain (+15.4 pp AllSF@5 over hybrid), while iterative Hop-2 adds a modest +2.8 pp that does not yield statistically significant exact-match improvements (McNemar Holm p = 1.0). Even with gold evidence, a 3B reader reaches only 53.0% EM; scaling the reader to 7B increases EM by +12.2 pp on identical evidence, establishing that the primary performance bottleneck is reader capacity rather than multi-hop retrieval.
